# TurnKey privacy policy — draft for review

**Not published. Prepared 16 October 2026.**

Operator and privacy contact supplied by the owner: Turnkey; thenovaxterior@gmail.com. Review the proposed practices below before publication, particularly retention, support access and overseas processing. No public policy has been deployed.

## Who we are

TurnKey is a sales and quoting platform for service businesses, available at turnkeycrm.net. It is operated by **Turnkey**, New Zealand. Contact our privacy officer at **thenovaxterior@gmail.com**.

This policy explains how TurnKey handles account information and information processed through connected sales tools. A service business using TurnKey remains responsible for explaining its own collection and use of its customers' information. If you submitted an enquiry to that business, contact it first about your enquiry or customer record. You may also contact us about TurnKey's handling of your information.

## Information collected and why

We process account names, email addresses, business settings and authentication information to provide accounts and business-specific access. Businesses and their authorised staff may enter customer names, contact details, property addresses, requested services, notes, communications, photographs, quotes and sales activity. These records support enquiry handling, quoting, follow-up and sales reporting.

When a business connects Wix forms, TurnKey receives the submitted lead information and submission identifiers. When it connects Meta, TurnKey may receive the Page and lead-form identifiers, lead answers and contact details made available by the permissions granted. Connected advertising reporting may include account, campaign, ad-set and ad identifiers, spend and performance statistics. Reports must not be taken to establish customer-level advertising attribution where that link is unavailable.

When Gmail is connected, TurnKey processes the messages and attachment information supported by that connection to show sales conversations and send messages requested by the user. Connected Google Maps services process addresses and location-related information for property measurements and travel estimates. When ServiceM8 is connected, supported customer, quote and job details may be transferred for authorised handoffs, and supported status or financial information may return to TurnKey.

Providing enquiry details is voluntary, but missing contact or property information may prevent the relevant business from responding or preparing a quote. Third-party connections are optional and can be disconnected.

## How information is obtained and used

Information comes from users, their staff, customers submitting forms, imported files and services connected by an authorised user. We use it to provide the requested platform features, maintain account security, troubleshoot errors and support users. A business is responsible for having an appropriate basis to upload its records, connect its services and communicate with its customers.

We use connected data to provide the features requested by the business, rather than to sell customer records or provide unrelated advertising. If a user requests optional AI assistance, the submitted prompt and included context may be sent to the configured AI provider, currently Anthropic. Users should avoid submitting information they are not authorised to share.

## Service providers and access

The platform uses Supabase for database and authentication services, Render for the backend and Netlify for website hosting. Optional connections may involve Meta, Wix, Google, ServiceM8 and notification providers. These providers receive information necessary for the features used and operate under their own terms and privacy policies. They may process information outside New Zealand. The locations and safeguards depend on the providers and services used. Contact us for details about the arrangements relevant to your account; we do not represent that all data is stored in New Zealand.

Information is accessible to the relevant business's authorised users and to service providers or support personnel where needed for the service, security or a lawful request. We may disclose information where legally required.

## Storage, security and retention

TurnKey uses account authentication, business-scoped access controls and server-side storage for integration credentials. No system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their accounts and restrict staff and integration permissions appropriately.

Our proposed retention approach is to keep information for the time needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes, then delete or anonymise it. Contact us to discuss deletion of account records and the applicable retention period. Deletion from backups or third-party systems may follow the relevant provider's retention process; immediate deletion of every copy is not promised. Disconnecting a provider stops the connection but does not by itself erase records already imported into the CRM or copies held by that provider.

## Access, correction and deletion requests

Contact **thenovaxterior@gmail.com** to request access to or correction of personal information, ask about deletion, or raise a privacy concern. Include enough information to identify the relevant business and records; do not email passwords or access tokens. We may verify your identity and authority before acting. If another business controls the records, we may direct your request to it or assist it in responding. Records may need to be retained for lawful purposes; we will explain applicable limits.

To stop future Meta access, disconnect Meta in TurnKey and remove the app through your Facebook business integration settings. To request deletion of imported Meta data, contact the privacy email above with the relevant business and Page ID.

If your concern remains unresolved, you can contact New Zealand's Office of the Privacy Commissioner at https://www.privacy.org.nz/.

## Browser storage and updates

TurnKey uses browser storage and authentication sessions to operate the app and retain settings. Connected providers may use their own cookies and storage. Browser and hosting services may also process technical request information to operate and secure the service. Any additional advertising or analytics tracking should be disclosed here before it is introduced.

We will update this policy when our practices change and show the effective date on the published page. Material changes should be communicated appropriately.
